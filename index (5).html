Music Requests on Twitch: A Viewer’s Guide

Music requests are one of the easiest ways to become part of a live DJ broadcast. A useful request gives the DJ a clear suggestion while leaving enough freedom to protect the flow of the set.

DJ FOLSOE MUSIC GUIDEPublished 7 August 2026Evergreen guide

How to send a request

On DJ FOLSOE streams, viewers can use a chat command such as !request Artist - Title. Danish and German variants may also be available during the broadcast.

What makes a strong request?

  • Include both artist and title.
  • Choose a record that fits the active show or theme.
  • Avoid sending the same suggestion repeatedly.
  • Remember that a request is a suggestion, not a guaranteed next track.

Why requests may not play immediately

A DJ set has tempo, key, mood and energy relationships. The requested record may work better later, or it may conflict with the current show format. Waiting for the right moment usually produces a better result than interrupting the mix.

Requests create community

The most enjoyable requests often come with a memory or a reason. That context can turn one song into a conversation shared by the whole chat.
