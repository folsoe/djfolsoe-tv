THE IDEA

The weekend has no fixed shape.

WEEKEND is deliberately a pop-up show.

It can land on a Friday evening when the working week is finished. It can appear on a Saturday afternoon when daylight, house music and absolutely no hurry seem like a good combination.

It can also become the natural home for a raidtrain slot when Twitch is moving as one long party from channel to channel.
