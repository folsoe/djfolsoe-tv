MORNING TRANSMISSION 01

Wake Up. Turn It Up. You Never Know When We Go Live.

Good Morning Twitch is DJ FOLSOE’s live morning show from Denmark — a relaxed, unpredictable and music-filled way to start the day with great songs, good company and absolutely no guarantee that everything will go according to plan.

This is not your traditional morning radio show.

There is no producer standing behind a glass wall counting down the seconds. No corporate playlist committee deciding what must be played next. And no alarm clock forcing DJ FOLSOE into the studio at exactly the same time every morning.

Good Morning Twitch happens when real life gives us a morning to enjoy.

DJ FOLSOE has a regular job outside Twitch, so the show has deliberately become something a little different: a morning pop-up show.

Whenever there is a day off, some unexpected free time, a holiday, a quiet morning or simply an opportunity to switch on the studio and play some music, Good Morning Twitch can suddenly appear LIVE.

And that unpredictability is part of the fun.

You might wake up, open Twitch and discover that the studio is already alive. The music is playing. The microphone is open. People are arriving in chat. Coffee is probably happening somewhere. And another Good Morning Twitch has begun.

02
MORNING TRANSMISSION 02

SATURDAY MORNINGS HAVE A SPECIAL PLACE

While weekday editions of Good Morning Twitch are spontaneous pop-up broadcasts, there is one morning that belongs naturally to the show: Saturday.

Every Saturday morning or during the Saturday morning hours, Good Morning Twitch becomes the soundtrack to the beginning of the weekend.

No weekday rush. No Monday morning panic. No need to pretend you are excited about an alarm clock.

Saturday gives us time. Time for another song. Time for requests. Time for conversations that go completely off-topic. And time to discover where the music takes us.

Some people are just waking up. Some are having breakfast. Some are already working. Some are driving. Some are cleaning the house. And somewhere in the world, somebody has probably been awake all night and is wondering why everyone else keeps saying “Good Morning.”

Everyone is welcome. That is exactly the point.

03
MORNING TRANSMISSION 03

THIS IS A MORNING SHOW BUILT AROUND MUSIC

At the heart of Good Morning Twitch is a simple idea: Start the day with music you actually want to hear.

The musical universe is deliberately broad.

Expect familiar radio hits from the 1980s, 1990s, 2000s, 2010s and today mixed with dance-pop, nu-disco, electronic music, forgotten favourites, selected classics and tracks that simply feel right for the morning.

One moment might bring back a song you have not heard for twenty years. The next might introduce something completely new. Then somebody in chat makes a request and suddenly the entire musical direction changes again.

That is live broadcasting. And that is exactly how we like it.

There is room for nostalgia. There is room for new music. There is room for guilty pleasures. And occasionally there is room for a song that makes everybody wonder: “Who requested THIS?”

Probably somebody in chat. Possibly the DJ. Nobody is admitting anything.

04
MORNING TRANSMISSION 04

RADIO MEETS TWITCH

Good Morning Twitch sits somewhere between a traditional radio morning show, a live DJ set and a Twitch community hanging out together.

DJ FOLSOE has always loved the atmosphere surrounding radio and broadcasting, so the microphone is an important part of the show.

This is not simply four hours of uninterrupted tracks.

There is conversation. There are greetings. There are music stories. There are requests. There are reactions from chat. There are spontaneous ideas. And there are those wonderfully unpredictable moments that can only happen during a genuinely live broadcast.

Sometimes we talk about music. Sometimes we talk about what is happening that morning. Sometimes somebody writes something in chat that sends the entire programme in a completely different direction.

That is not a technical problem. That is the programme.

05
MORNING TRANSMISSION 05

THE COMMUNITY IS PART OF THE SHOW

Good Morning Twitch would not really be Good Morning Twitch without the people watching it.

The viewers are not treated as an audience sitting quietly on the other side of the screen. They are part of what happens.

Chat can influence the atmosphere, react to what is playing and — when music requests are open — suggest tracks they would like to hear.

Regular viewers meet new viewers. People arrive from different countries. Some stay for hours. Some listen while getting ready for work. Others disappear for twenty minutes and return with breakfast.

And then there are the legendary lurkers. We know you are out there. You do not have to say anything. Listening counts too.

The idea is to create the feeling of switching on a small international music station where familiar people gradually appear during the morning. You can participate as much — or as little — as you want.

06
MORNING TRANSMISSION 06

A MORNING SHOW WITHOUT BORDERS

Good Morning Twitch broadcasts from Denmark, but Twitch does not really have borders.

Morning in Denmark is already later in the day somewhere else and still the middle of the night somewhere further west. That creates a wonderfully strange international breakfast table.

Someone in Denmark might be drinking their first cup of coffee. Someone elsewhere in Europe may already have been working for hours. Another viewer may be joining from a completely different time zone where “Good Morning” makes absolutely no sense.

We do not mind. Good Morning Twitch is less about what your clock says and more about the atmosphere.

If you have just woken up, welcome. If you are working, welcome. If it is lunchtime where you live, welcome.

If it is midnight and you have somehow ended up watching a Danish amateur DJ playing dance-pop on Twitch... Honestly, welcome. You have probably found the right place.

07
MORNING TRANSMISSION 07

EXPECT THE UNEXPECTED

Because many editions are pop-up broadcasts, Good Morning Twitch is intentionally allowed to evolve from one show to another.

There is a basic musical identity, but there is no requirement that every morning must sound exactly like the previous one.

A show might begin quietly with familiar radio favourites and gradually become more energetic. Another morning might start with nu-disco.

Someone makes a request. A classic appears. Chat reacts. Another track suddenly reminds DJ FOLSOE of something from the 1980s. And twenty minutes later we are somewhere completely different from where we started.

Perfect.

Good Morning Twitch is not supposed to feel programmed by an algorithm. It is supposed to feel live.

08
MORNING TRANSMISSION 08

WHEN DOES GOOD MORNING TWITCH GO LIVE?

There are two ways to catch the show.

Saturday mornings and Saturday mornings/forenoons are the regular home of Good Morning Twitch.

But during the week, things become much less predictable.

Because DJ FOLSOE also has a regular job, additional editions appear whenever work schedules, days off, holidays and real life create an opportunity for a morning broadcast.

That means an unexpected weekday morning can suddenly become a Good Morning Twitch morning. Think of those editions as bonus broadcasts.

No unnecessary rules. No pretending that a hobby is a multinational television network.

Just: “I have the morning free. Shall we play some music?” Yes. We shall.

09
MORNING TRANSMISSION 09

WHY GOOD MORNING TWITCH EXISTS

Good Morning Twitch is ultimately about something very simple. Music can completely change the beginning of a day.

One familiar song can bring back a memory. One unexpected track can improve your mood. One ridiculous conversation in chat can make you laugh.

And sometimes having a group of people somewhere in the background makes an ordinary Saturday morning feel just a little less ordinary.

That is what Good Morning Twitch is trying to create.

Not perfection. Not celebrity DJ culture. Not a nightclub at eight o'clock in the morning. Although, admittedly, things occasionally move dangerously close to that last one.

It is simply a place for people who enjoy music. A place where radio culture meets Twitch. A place where old favourites meet new discoveries. And a place where the person behind the DJ desk is enjoying the music just as much as everyone watching.

10
MORNING TRANSMISSION 10

YOUR MORNING. OUR MUSIC.

So whether you are making breakfast, driving, working from home, cleaning the kitchen, recovering from Friday night, pretending to exercise, actually exercising or simply refusing to leave the sofa... Good Morning Twitch can be your soundtrack.

Drop into chat. Say hello. Request a song when requests are open. Or simply leave the stream running and enjoy the music.

There is no dress code. There is no guest list. And there is definitely no requirement to be fully awake.

Because Good Morning Twitch is partly a pop-up show, the best morning broadcasts are sometimes the ones you did not know were coming.

So keep an eye on the DJ FOLSOE schedule. Watch for the LIVE signal. And when the studio suddenly wakes up...

Come in. Turn it up. And Good Morning, Twitch.
