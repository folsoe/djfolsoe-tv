THE THURSDAY NIGHT FORMULA

Two hours.
One genre.
No apologies.

Every Thursday from 19:00 to 21:00 Copenhagen time, DJ FOLSOE turns the stream over to Eurodance.

Not a mixed nostalgia show. Not “a bit of 90s”. Not a playlist where Eurodance gets three songs and politely leaves again.

This show is built around the genre itself.

From the early 1990s explosion through the late-90s peak, the 2000s evolution and the records that carried the DNA forward afterwards.

For two hours, Eurodance gets the whole room.

03 · THE MAXI-CD YEARS

When one song needed five versions.

Radio Edit. Extended Mix. Club Mix. Dub. Instrumental. Remix by somebody who apparently had access to even more synthesizers.

Eurodance belonged to an era where a single could be an event in itself.

The artwork was loud. The typography was questionable. The gradients were fearless. The track list was longer than strictly necessary.

It was magnificent.

04 · 1990 → FORWARD

The sound changed.
The energy stayed.

1990–1993 THE IGNITION

House, techno, hip-house, rave and pop start colliding into something new.

1994–1997 THE EXPLOSION

Eurodance becomes unavoidable. Radio, clubs, charts, compilation CDs, everywhere.

1998–2001 THE PEAK YEARS

Bigger productions, sharper pop instincts and hooks engineered with industrial precision.

2002–2009 THE EVOLUTION

The sound mutates into hands up, vocal dance, trance-pop and new club formats.

2010 → NOW THE DNA LIVES ON

The names change. The production changes. The core idea still turns up everywhere.

05 · BUILT ACROSS EUROPE

A continental dancefloor.

Eurodance was never one city, one label or one country.

Germany, Italy, the Netherlands, Belgium, Sweden, Denmark and the rest of Europe all fed different ideas into the sound.

Some productions were sleek. Some were raw. Some were strange. Some were enormous hits. Some disappeared after one brilliant single and became cult favourites thirty years later.

That mixture is exactly why the genre is still fun to dig through.

07 · THE CHORUS DEPARTMENT

If you can remember it after thirty years,
the hook probably worked.

Eurodance never suffered from a fear of choruses.

The best tracks were designed to make sense within seconds. A melody arrives. A phrase sticks. By the second chorus, the room knows what to do.

This was pop music with club shoes on.

08 · YOUR TRACK CAN CHANGE THE NIGHT

Requests are dangerous.
That is why we like them.

When requests are open, Twitch chat can help shape the show.

Maybe it is a giant anthem everybody knows. Maybe it is a Scandinavian track that deserved more attention. Maybe it is a one-hit wonder that instantly unlocks a memory nobody knew they still had.

Or maybe it is something so magnificently 1990s that the artwork alone should be placed in a museum.

Put it in the request queue.

09 · BEYOND THE OBVIOUS HITS

We are going deeper than the compilation CD.

Of course the huge records belong here. A Eurodance show without the major anthems would be suspicious.

But the fun begins when we go beyond the first ten tracks everybody remembers.

Regional hits. Scandinavian releases. Follow-up singles. Strange remixes. Records that were massive in one country and nearly invisible in another. Artists who had one hit. Artists who had twenty.

Two hours gives us room to remember more than the obvious.
