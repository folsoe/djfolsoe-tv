01

THE JOURNEY STARTS HERE

Trance is not background music.

Trance Tuesday is DJ FOLSOE’s dedicated live journey through uplifting, progressive and classic trance.

It is music that takes its time. A set can breathe. A melody can arrive slowly. A breakdown can hold the room still for thirty seconds before everything opens again.

That is the point.

02 · UPLIFTING

That moment before everything lifts.

Big melodies. Emotional breakdowns. Long builds. A few seconds where the kick disappears and the entire track seems to float.

Then the energy comes back.

Not subtle. Not apologetic. Exactly as intended.

03 · PROGRESSIVE

Movement without hurry.

Progressive trance does not need to tell you where it is going. It changes slowly enough that you only notice the transformation after it has already happened.

Deeper grooves. Gradual tension. Small details that matter more the longer you stay with them.

04 · THE BREAKDOWN

Everything disappears.
Except the melody.

For a moment, there is almost nothing left.

No kick. No rush. No need to get anywhere.

Just space.

Twitch chat may briefly become quiet. No guarantees.

05 · CLASSIC TRANCE

Some records never became old.

MEMORY

The classics are here because some melodies still work instantly, even decades later.

You hear the first few notes and suddenly you remember a club, a festival, a CD, a radio show, a friend, a night, a car journey or simply a version of yourself from years ago.

That is not nostalgia as decoration. That is part of what trance does.

09 · LIVE ON TWITCH

The route can change.

When requests are open, viewers can help steer the journey.

Some people know every label, remix and catalogue number. Some simply know when something gives them goosebumps.

Both are welcome.

You do not need to prove anything before joining the chat.

Come for the trance. Stay because somebody just remembered a track you had completely forgotten.

10 · THE JOURNEY

A good set should take you somewhere.

Beginning. Direction. Tension. Space. Release. Another climb.

Sometimes euphoric. Sometimes deeper. Sometimes slightly off-route because Twitch chat had an idea.

That is live music.
