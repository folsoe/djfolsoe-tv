01

TRANSMISSION 01 · THE IDEA

Tuesday disappears.
The music takes over.

Trance Tuesday is DJ FOLSOE’s dedicated live trance show on Twitch.

This is the night where melody gets room to breathe, builds are allowed to build, breakdowns are allowed to stop time for a moment, and the payoff is worth waiting for.

No frantic genre-hopping. No need to squeeze everything into three minutes. Trance works differently.

It takes its time.

And so do we.

02

TRANSMISSION 02

Uplifting.

Big melodies. Long builds. Emotional breakdowns. That moment when the kick disappears, the room opens up and everybody knows something is about to happen.

Then it lands.

This is the side of trance that makes people close their eyes before they put their hands in the air.

03

TRANSMISSION 03

Progressive.

Deeper. Slower to reveal itself. A groove that gradually changes shape while you are inside it.

Progressive trance is less interested in announcing where it is going.

It would rather take you there first.

04

TRANSMISSION 04

Classics.

The records that became memories. The melodies you recognise before your brain has managed to locate the title.

Some tracks simply refuse to become old.

We are not going to argue with them.

THE BREAKDOWN

For a moment,
nothing needs to happen.

One of the best things about trance is the confidence to remove almost everything. The drums disappear. The melody rises. Time stretches.

And for a few seconds, Twitch chat may even stop typing.

We said may.

06

TRANSMISSION 06

Requests can change the flight path.

When requests are open, the audience can help steer the journey.

Maybe you remember a classic. Maybe there is a new track we should hear. Maybe one melody has been sitting in your head all day and now everybody else needs to deal with it too.

Fair enough.

Trance Tuesday is live. The route can change.

07

TRANSMISSION 07

Chat is part of the atmosphere.

Some viewers know every release, label and remix.

Some just know when something sounds good.

Both are equally welcome.

You do not need to pass an entrance exam in trance history before saying hello.

Come for the music. Stay for the people who understand why a seven-minute track is perfectly reasonable.

TRANSMISSION 08 · THE JOURNEY

A set should go somewhere.

Trance Tuesday is not a bag of unrelated tracks placed next to each other.

The best sets have direction. A beginning. Tension. Space. Release. Peaks. A moment to breathe. Then another climb.

Sometimes the destination is euphoric.

Sometimes it is deeper.

Sometimes we take a slight detour because somebody in Twitch chat had an idea.

That is live radio logic.

Except this is Twitch.
