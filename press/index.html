OFFICIAL CHANNEL INFORMATION

DJ FOLSOE press and channel information

DJ FOLSOE is the host and DJ behind FOLSOE TV, a Danish broadcast-style website and Twitch music channel built around themed live shows, music discovery and community participation.

Channel description

FOLSOE TV presents recurring programmes across Eurodance, trance, retro hits, nu-disco, dance-pop and radio favourites. Viewers can discover the schedule on the website and continue to the live Twitch channel.
