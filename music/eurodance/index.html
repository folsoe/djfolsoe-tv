DJ FOLSOE MUSIC GUIDE

Eurodance

Eurodance combines high-energy electronic production, powerful vocal hooks and the unmistakable momentum of 1990s and early-2000s dance culture. On FOLSOE TV, the genre appears in dedicated broadcasts, themed sets and viewer requests.

What defines the Eurodance sound?

Typical tracks balance driving four-on-the-floor rhythms with melodic choruses, synth hooks and contrasting vocal styles. The strongest records work equally well as nostalgic memories and immediate dance-floor music.
