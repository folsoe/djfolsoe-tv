DJ FOLSOE MUSIC GUIDE

Nu-Disco

Nu-disco translates the warmth, basslines and rhythmic confidence of classic disco into contemporary electronic production. It can feel familiar without becoming a recreation of the past.

Where classic groove meets modern production

Funk-inspired bass, filtered textures, polished vocals and club-ready arrangements make nu-disco a natural bridge between retro programming and current dance music.
