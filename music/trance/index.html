DJ FOLSOE MUSIC GUIDE

Trance Music

Trance is built around progression: evolving melodies, extended builds, emotional breakdowns and releases designed to feel larger than the individual track. DJ FOLSOE uses that journey as the backbone of Trance Tuesday.

From classic trance to uplifting energy

The show can move between recognised classics, progressive moments and uplifting records while preserving a coherent musical story rather than treating the genre as a random playlist.
