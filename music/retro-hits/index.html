DJ FOLSOE MUSIC GUIDE

Retro Hits

Retro Hits celebrates records that remain instantly recognisable across generations. The focus is not limited to one decade: disco, pop, rock, dance and radio favourites can all belong when the songs still connect with a live audience.

More than nostalgia

A strong retro set uses contrast, pacing and shared memories. Familiar choruses create participation, while less obvious selections give listeners something to rediscover.
