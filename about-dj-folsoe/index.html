THE PERSON BEHIND FOLSOE TV

About DJ FOLSOE

DJ FOLSOE is a Danish DJ and live-stream host presenting themed music shows for an international Twitch audience. FOLSOE TV brings the schedule, show identities and music discovery together in one broadcast-style website.

Music, broadcasting and community

The channel combines decades of music interest with modern live interaction. Viewers can join the chat, request music during supported shows and return for recurring programmes with their own sound and visual identity.
